PROJECT OVERVIEWProject Name[Final Brand Name — To Be Confirmed]Product TypeMobile Business Management & Inventory Management ApplicationPlatformAndroidiOSBuilt with Flutter + DartOne-Line DescriptionA simple, modern mobile business management application that allows a business owner to manage products, inventory, suppliers, incoming and outgoing stock, sales, purchases, receipts, expenses, barcode scanning, and daily cash from one place.Primary GoalThe primary goal is to give a business owner a single, simple control center for managing day-to-day business operations without requiring complicated accounting or inventory software.The application should make common tasks such as:Checking stockAdding productsReceiving stockRecording sales/outgoing stockManaging suppliersPaying suppliersRecording expensesScanning productsGenerating receiptsTracking daily cashReviewing reportsfast and straightforward.Product PhilosophyThe application should follow:Simple → Fast → ReliableThe admin should be able to complete common actions with as few taps as reasonably possible.The system should prioritize accurate interconnected data over unnecessary features.2. FINAL BUILD PRINCIPLESPrinciple 1 — Keep It SimpleDo not introduce unnecessary accounting complexity into the MVP.Principle 2 — One Source of TruthInventory, cash, supplier balances, receipts, and reports must derive from the underlying transaction records.Principle 3 — Never Silently Change DataImportant inventory and financial changes must be traceable.Principle 4 — Transactions Must Be AtomicA transaction should either complete successfully or roll back safely.Principle 5 — Offline FirstThe core business workflow should not depend on a continuous internet connection.Principle 6 — Mobile FirstThe interface must be optimized for touch, one-handed use where practical, and common Android/iOS screen sizes.Principle 7 — Fast Common ActionsScanning, stock-in, stock-out, expenses, and supplier payments should require minimal navigation.Principle 8 — No Unrequested FeaturesFeatures outside the defined MVP must not be automatically introduced by the implementation tool.50.
